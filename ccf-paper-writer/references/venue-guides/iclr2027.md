# ICLR 2027 Conference Guide

Verified: 2026-09-12. Applies to the conference paper track, not workshops. Recheck official sources before a final submission decision, especially later discussion and camera-ready instructions. Source records: `iclr-2027-author-guidelines`, `iclr-2027-ai-policy`, `iclr-2027-call-for-papers`, and `iclr-2027-style-files` in `ccf-common/references/source-registry.yaml`.

## Official rules and sources

- [Author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines): anonymity, page limits, supplementary material, authorship, submission and discussion rules.
- [AI policy for authors](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors): paper and submission-form disclosure of actual AI use, with authors responsible for the final work.
- [Call for papers](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers): dates and scope.
- [Official style archive](https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip): layout and statement examples. Unmodified extracted files are bundled in `ccf-latex-templates/ICLR/2027/`.

The author page contains inconsistent legacy FAQ wording about page limits and title changes. Its main formatting section explicitly sets 9 pages for initial submission and 10 for discussion/camera-ready. Use that stage-specific rule, not an isolated FAQ phrase. For a later title/author change where current official instructions still conflict, mark the decision unresolved and direct the author to the chairs; do not promise it is permitted.

## Template and build

Use [the 2027 template directory](../../../ccf-latex-templates/ICLR/2027) at the installed sibling location. All seven official files travel together. The `.tex` is a formatting example, not a research manuscript or a required scientific section outline. Preserve existing verified 2027 project templates and build configuration instead of overwriting them.

The style uses a **single-column** article layout. Size tables against the actual `\linewidth`; do not impose a generic 7.5 cm two-column budget, use `\twocoltitle`, or alter margins/font sizes to fit more content. Use commands defined by the supplied style and packages.

For a new document, the essential style setup is:

```latex
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{iclr2027_conference,times}
\usepackage{hyperref}
\usepackage{url}
% Keep \iclrfinalcopy disabled for submission and anonymous review.
% Enable it only for an accepted camera-ready version.
```

Use `\bibliographystyle{iclr2027_conference}` and the project's own `.bib`, with `\citet`/`\citep`. Do not import the sample bibliography as research evidence. Keep the official style files unchanged. Compile through the existing project command; for a new standalone template, `latexmk -pdf iclr2027_conference.tex` works from the copied template directory. Build outside the installed skill's asset directory.

## Anonymity and stage

| Stage | Main-text limit | Identity in manuscript |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial submission | 9 pages | Anonymous, including supplementary material |
| Discussion/rebuttal revision | 10 pages | Keep anonymous through review |
| Accepted camera-ready | 10 pages | Apply final author information and current final instructions |

For anonymous versions inspect the rendered author block, acknowledgments, identifying affiliations/links, supplementary files, and PDF metadata. Keep `\iclrfinalcopy` disabled. The authors must still enter their real identities in the **OpenReview author fields**; those fields are distinct from the anonymous PDF. Cite relevant own public work in third person. Existing arXiv papers do not by themselves break anonymity.

## Page accounting and statements

The main-text budget includes title, abstract, scientific text, figures, and tables. References and appendices are excluded; appendices follow the bibliography. Essential evidence belongs in the main text because reviewers need not read appendices. Do not equate total PDF page count with main-text page count.

Place the following statements after the main text and before references, following the official template's unnumbered subsection style:

| Statement | Status | Accounting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `\subsection*{AI use statement}` | Required | Excluded; the template limits it to 1 page |
| `\subsection*{Ethics statement}` | Recommended | Excluded; at most 1 page |
| `\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}` | Recommended | Excluded; normally a paragraph pointing to relevant material |

Draft AI disclosure from the author's actual use of tools and verification activities, consistent with the submission form. Do not claim that AI was unused, that every output was checked, or that a specified number of authors verified code without supplied evidence. Keep unknown disclosure facts as draft placeholders and flag them before submission. The template's boilerplate is illustrative, not a factual statement to copy verbatim. Preserve mandatory disclosure during polishing or compression.

## Dates and author actions

| Milestone | AoE (UTC−12) | Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract | 2026-09-18 23:59 | 2026-09-19 19:59 |
| Full paper and supplements | 2026-09-25 23:59 | 2026-09-26 19:59 |

Use a genuine abstract; placeholders are not accepted. Finalize the author set before the abstract deadline. Author order may change until the full-paper deadline; adding or removing authors after the abstract deadline is disallowed by the author FAQ. Check OpenReview profiles, applicable reciprocal-review obligations, and submission quotas against the current official page with the authors. Do not infer author eligibility from the manuscript or treat these account checks as a LaTeX build result.

## Checks by task

- **Writing or compression:** retain scientific content and actual uncertainty; preserve citation keys, labels, equations and required statements. A section outline or fixed seed count is a research choice, not an ICLR formatting rule.
- **Template setup:** use the full 2027 asset set and verify a fresh build. Existing manuscripts, bibliographies and project paths take precedence over example filenames.
- **Scientific review:** distinguish missing supplied evidence from a demonstrated flaw. This guide does not define official reviewer scores; verify the current reviewer guide if an official rubric is requested.
- **Submission check:** identify the exact stage; inspect source and fresh PDF, main-text boundaries, statements, anonymous supplements/metadata, and missing references. Report pass, fail, not applicable, or unverified per check, with source/date for policy conclusions. A successful build is not a complete readiness verdict.
- **Rebuttal/camera-ready:** recheck current instructions, retain anonymity during review, and apply the appropriate 10-page budget. Submission preparation does not authorize external upload.
